TRT for women

Reclaiming your vitality: understanding female testosterone

Testosterone is not just a "male" hormone — it is a powerful vitality hormone essential for women's health, mood, and desire. It works alongside estrogen and progesterone to keep you feeling energized and well.

What is testosterone's role in women?

Think of testosterone as the hormone that fuels your drive, both physically and mentally. Produced in small amounts by your ovaries and adrenal glands, it is crucial for:

FunctionThe benefit to you
Sexual desire (libido)The primary driver of your sexual thoughts and motivation.
Energy and focusContributes to your overall energy levels, motivation, and mental clarity (combating "brain fog").
Muscle and bone healthHelps you build and maintain lean muscle mass and is important for strong bones (bone density).
Mood and well-beingCan play a role in confidence, sense of well-being, and reduction of anxiety and irritability.

Why does my testosterone decrease?

A decline in testosterone is a natural part of aging, but a significant drop can lead to noticeable symptoms.

  • Natural agingYour testosterone levels begin to gradually decrease in your late 20s and early 30s.
  • MenopauseWhile not as sudden as the drop in estrogen, testosterone continues its decline, with levels often falling further after menopause.
  • Ovary removal (oophorectomy)Since the ovaries are the main source of testosterone, surgical removal can cause an immediate, sharp drop.
  • MedicationsCertain medications (like some oral estrogen therapies or SSRI antidepressants) can interfere with your body's ability to utilize or produce testosterone.

Treatment for women: what to know

TRT for women is about restoring your testosterone to a healthy, normal female range to alleviate these specific symptoms. It is a highly individualized therapy.

  • IndicationThe strongest evidence for TRT in women is for treating Hypoactive Sexual Desire Disorder (HSDD) — when a low sex drive causes distress.
  • DosageTRT for women uses a much lower dose than that prescribed for men (often about one tenth the male dose). The goal is to return you to your body's optimal female range, not to elevate levels above normal.
  • FormsThe most common and easily managed forms are topical creams, gels, or injections. Some clinics also offer pellets (small implants placed under the skin).
  • MonitoringRegular blood testing is essential to ensure your levels stay within the therapeutic female range and to prevent side effects.

Are my symptoms due to low testosterone?

If you feel "off" and are experiencing a loss of your personal drive, it may be a sign your testosterone levels are low. Common symptoms include:

  • Low libido — a noticeable drop in interest, thoughts, or fantasies about sex.
  • Unexplained fatigue — feeling constantly run down, tired, or lacking the stamina you once had.
  • Brain fog — difficulty concentrating, poor memory, or a general lack of mental sharpness.
  • Muscle loss — finding it harder to maintain muscle tone or losing strength even with consistent exercise.
  • Mood changes — increased feelings of anxiety, low mood, or a loss of your usual zest for life.

Potential side effects

Usually due to excessive dosing:

  • Acne or oily skin
  • Increased growth of body or facial hair
  • Thinning hair on the scalp
  • Rarely: deepening of the voice or clitoral enlargement

Next steps

If you are experiencing any of these symptoms, the first step is to schedule a consultation. Your provider will take a comprehensive look at your health, symptoms, and check your current hormone levels to determine if TRT is the right path to help you reclaim your energy and desire.

Anti-aging

Discover the benefits of NAD+

NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) supports your cells at the most fundamental level — energy production, repair, and resilience.

What are the benefits of NAD+?

  • Cellular rejuvenationEnhances mitochondrial function, ATP production, and DNA repair.
  • Cognitive improvementsSupports mental clarity, memory, and focus while reducing neuroinflammation.
  • Physical performanceBoosts muscle recovery, exercise endurance, and reaction times.
  • Metabolic healthAids weight management and reduces age-related metabolic decline.
  • Cardiovascular benefitsMay lower blood pressure, improve vascular stiffness, and reduce inflammation.
  • Mood regulationIncreases serotonin levels, potentially alleviating symptoms of depression and anxiety.

How fast will I see results?

  • HoursNAD+ levels begin rising, supporting cellular processes like energy production.
  • 3 daysMitochondrial replication increases, enhancing energy metabolism.
  • 1–2 weeksImproved mental clarity, energy, and sleep patterns become noticeable.
  • 30 daysNAD+ levels rise by roughly 40%, with measurable metabolic and cardiovascular benefits.
  • 6 weeksMuscle strength and recovery improve in older adults.
  • 3–6 monthsFunctional benefits like faster gait speed and long-term anti-aging effects.

Side effects

NAD+ therapy is generally considered safe for most people, but side effects can occur temporarily. The most frequently reported side effects include nausea, fatigue, headache, diarrhea, flushing of the skin, and palpitations after administration.

Concierge medicine

Concierge medicine is a membership-based healthcare model that offers personalized and direct access to physicians in exchange for an annual or monthly fee. This approach allows patients to enjoy benefits such as 24/7 access to doctors via telehealth, same-day or next-day appointments, longer consultations, and minimal wait times.

Physicians typically limit their patient roster to provide more individualized care and build stronger relationships with patients. Services often include comprehensive wellness evaluations, preventive care, and coordination with specialists.
